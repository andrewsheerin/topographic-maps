Route the decision at hand per the current tier:

**If it's mine** (or the tier says stop): full entry under **Needs you** in
`project-management/DECISIONS.md` — D-n, status OPEN, what it blocks, 2–3 sentences of context,
an options table with real trade-offs and reversibility, and your recommendation. Do the analysis
before writing; a bare question is not an entry. Then say what you're doing meanwhile.

**If the tier lets you decide:** decide, add one line to the **Decision Log** (date, decision,
why), and keep moving. No ceremony.

When in doubt about which lane, it's mine.
